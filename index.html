BALL ESCAPE

Generate animated ball escape videos.

Ball Escape is a desktop application that generates short vertical animations with a moving ball, concentric rings, effects and a timer.

Terms of Service Privacy Policy

What Ball Escape does

The application runs on a desktop computer and creates video files from user-selected generation settings. Generated files can then be published manually or shared through supported platform integrations.

TikTok integration

An optional TikTok integration can allow a user to authorize the application to publish generated video content through TikTok's official developer APIs. TikTok account authentication and platform data are handled according to TikTok's own policies and the permissions granted by the user.
